About this mockup

Real: forecast (Open-Meteo, Pearland, mornings pulled Sat Nov 14 at 11:30 PM, evenings Sun Nov 15 at 1:30 AM), eBird notable reports for Nov 8 to 14, tide predictions (NOAA station 8771450, Galveston Pier 21), sunrise times, and PFD A shift days from your calendar (off at 6:00 AM).

Estimated: first light and last light are set 25 minutes from sunrise and sunset; the build computes civil twilight properly.

Placeholder: the spot list is a first candidate set, drive times are rough estimates from central Pearland, and the scoring weights are a first guess. Every spot uses the Pearland forecast.

Score: starts at 100, loses points for sustained wind above 6 mph, gusts above 20 mph, rain chance, and heavy cloud. Tidal spots gain or lose for whether low tide lands in the morning.
